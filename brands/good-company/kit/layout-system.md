# Layout system

- **Logo top left**, plum on cream, never on a dark band. About a fifth of the width.
- **Headline stacked** on two or three lines down the left half, as big as the lines allow. Line breaks at natural phrases ("Holiday / Listening / Party").
- **Photos are prints** on the right half: a cream border, 2 to 4 degrees of tilt, one overlapping the other, tape on a corner. Never a full-bleed rectangle.
- **The date** is set big in the heading font; the time sits in a red box under it.
- **Details** run along the bottom as a row of three, each with a small mark, divided by thin plum rules. A painted band may sit behind them.
- Leave 56px safe margin; nothing important touches an edge.
