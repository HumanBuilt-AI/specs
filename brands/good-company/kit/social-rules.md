# Social rules

- Canvas: 1080 x 1080 (1:1) or 1080 x 1350 (4:5). A flyer is the 4:5 layout scaled up.
- The logo appears once, top left, on cream.
- Headline at least 64px; body at least 30px.
- No hashtags or emoji on the post itself.
