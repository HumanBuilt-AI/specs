# Art direction

- Warm light, wood, records. Grade photos slightly warm and a touch faded, like a print that has been on the wall a while.
- Texture belongs to the paper (grain, speckle, worn ink), never smeared over people's faces.
- Illustration is simple and printed: one or two flat colors from the palette, like a screen print.
- The logo is never recolored except plum to cream on a dark ground. Never redrawn.
