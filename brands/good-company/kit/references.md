# References

What the three share: **one big idea per poster**, a record as the hero, confident type, and a lot of restraint. None of them is busy. Borrow that over any single detail.

1. **Damn the Man (Empire Records)**, `references/01-damn-the-man.webp`. Borrow: the headline as a wall of tight, heavy type filling the width; one huge red record as the hero, overlapping the type; worn screen-print texture on the record; small details set in a quiet row at the bottom. Ignore: its words, credits and logo.
2. **Onsite Records post**, `references/02-onsite-records.webp`. Borrow: a candid photo that feels real (flash, film grain, warmth); a confident sentence-case headline in a clean sans; a small brand line under it. Ignore: the person, its words, and type set straight on the photo (our rules keep copy on a ground).
3. **Vinyl Kind of People**, `references/03-vinyl-kind-of-people.webp`. Borrow: a flat geometric record drawn in concentric lines with one bold color ring; lots of empty space; small spaced capitals as the only line of text. Ignore: its words and the dark ground (Good Company is cream paper).

Never copy a reference's words, logo, people or products.
