# Energy

**Level: 3 of 5 (friendly shop flyer).** Scale: 1 is a gallery card, 3 is a flyer by the register, 5 is a neon sign at 2am.

The reason: people come for the records and stay because it feels like someone's living room. The posts should feel handmade and printed, never like an app screen.

## What 3 means here

- Paper you can feel: grain, a little ink speckle, slightly worn edges on the big type. A flat cream rectangle reads as a template.
- One big serif headline that fills the width, set tight, two colors at most (plum plus one red word).
- Photos as prints: white or cream borders, a slight tilt, overlapping, a strip of tape.
- Small illustrated touches that belong to a record shop: a vinyl record edge, a sleeve, a seasonal sprig for a holiday event. One or two, never a sticker pile.
- Details as a tidy row with small icons or red dots, plum on cream.

## Too far

- Glow, neon, spray paint, more than two tilted things.
- More than three colors on one post.
- Painted or invented people, store fronts or signs. Photos come from the owner.
