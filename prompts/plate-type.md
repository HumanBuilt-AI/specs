# System

## Type over a painted plate

This time an image model will paint the art under your type: paper, texture, torn shapes, illustration (a record, a seasonal sprig, a painted band). You design the type layer it will sit under, and you place the photo and the logo.

- Lay out every slot as if the plate were already there. Text sits on plain paper or on your own solid panels, never on the painted art, so the plate needs calm areas behind every slot.
- Leave the post's own background as `var(--role-background)`; the plate covers it. Keep your panels few: the plate brings the texture, the color bands and the illustration, so don't draw them in CSS.
- Leave room for art: at least one area of the canvas (a corner, a band, a gap beside the headline) with no slot in it, where the plate paints the illustration.
- Still use `var(--ink-wear)` on the big type, so the real type looks printed like the plate.
- Add a rationale entry with `decision: "art zones"` that tells the painter, in plain words, where its illustration and color go and what each one is (for example "a vinyl record bleeding off the bottom left corner; a deep green painted band across the bottom 22% behind the details; a holly sprig right of 'Party'"). Use only the kit's colors in that description, plus at most one seasonal color the brief calls for.
